## On-field SFX (`sfx/`)

Every source below was checked on its own page at download time: Freesound pages showed the license link "Creative Commons 0" (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/); the Kenney page lists "Creative Commons CC0" and the zip ships a License.txt saying "Creative Commons Zero, CC0". CC0 needs no attribution; we keep this record for provenance. Freesound audio is the site's public HQ preview MP3 (`cdn.freesound.org/previews/...-hq.mp3`); Kenney audio comes from `kenney_impact-sounds.zip` (OGG).

Processing tools: macOS `afconvert` (decode to 16-bit/44.1 kHz WAV, encode AAC-LC .m4a, 80 kbps mono) plus a small Node script (slice, playback-speed variation, layering, mono sum, 60-150 Hz high-pass, silence trim, peak/RMS normalisation, fades). Composite files list every layer.
